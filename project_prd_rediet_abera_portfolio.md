## 3. Product & Design Architecture

### 3.1 Design System & Aesthetic Foundation
- **Visual Identity:** Clean, clinical-editorial aesthetic conveying trust, precision, warmth, and institutional credibility.
- **Color Palette:**
  - *Primary Tone:* Deep Slate Navy (`#1e293b`) for authority and typography.
  - *Accent Tone:* Mint / Clinical Teal (`#0d9488` / `#14b8a6`) signifying healthcare, vitality, and precision.
  - *Backgrounds:* Crisp off-white and cool surface containers (`#f7f9fb`, `#ffffff`).
- **Typography:** Newsreader / Editorial Serif for confident section headers balanced with high-legibility sans-serif for structured metadata, tables, and credentials.
